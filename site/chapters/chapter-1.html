Chapter 1: Futures Basics — Contracts, Exchanges, and Why Futures Exist

Core analogy: A futures contract is like reserving a hotel room for a future date at today’s agreed price.

What you will learn

  • What a futures contract is.
  • Who uses futures (hedgers, speculators).
  • How CME and other exchanges standardize contracts.

Lesson notes

This chapter is written for absolute beginners, using plain language before jargon. Read once for understanding, then reread and extract your rules into a personal playbook.

At the end of this chapter, write 3 “if-then” rules (example: “If volatility expands into major news, then I reduce size by 50%”).

Chapter 1 Test (5 Questions)

  1. In your own words, what is the main concept of this chapter?
  2. Which analogy best explains this topic and why?
  3. Name one common beginner mistake for this topic.
  4. What rule can prevent that mistake?
  5. How will you apply this concept in your next simulated trade?
Answer guide

Strong answers are specific, measurable, and tied to risk control. Avoid vague statements like “be careful.” Use exact thresholds and conditions.
