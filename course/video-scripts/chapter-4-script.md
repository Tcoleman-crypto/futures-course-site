# Chapter 4 Video Script — Price Action Fundamentals

## Runtime
10-15 minutes

## Hook (0:00-0:30)
"In this lesson, you'll understand Price Action Fundamentals using a simple analogy so you can make safer, clearer trading decisions."

## Core Analogy
Explain the concept like teaching a friend with zero finance knowledge. Keep one central analogy and tie each technical term back to it.

## Teaching Segments
1. **Concept in plain English**
2. **Technical definition**
3. **Live chart or visual example**
4. **Common beginner mistake**
5. **One risk control rule**

## On-Screen Prompts
- "Pause and write your rule."
- "Say it back in your own words."
- "Would this protect your account in a losing streak?"

## Close CTA
"Complete the chapter test before moving to the next lesson."
