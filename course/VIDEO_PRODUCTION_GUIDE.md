# Video Production Guide (Fast Execution)

You can produce each chapter video in under 1 hour:

1. Use `course/video-scripts/chapter-*-script.md` as narration.
2. Build slides from `course/slides/chapter-*-slides.md` (Canva/Google Slides).
3. Record voiceover (Loom, OBS, Screen Studio, Descript).
4. Export MP4 and upload to your course platform.

## AI Video Option
- Generate voice from script using ElevenLabs.
- Create slide visuals with Canva Magic Design.
- Assemble in CapCut or Descript.
